Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: Muduco does not track you and shows no ads. Your lesson progress stays only on your device. Data from your microphone and camera never leaves your device. The app only fetches announcements and app settings from our own server, and sends anonymous aggregate counts that do not identify you (you can turn this off in Settings). If you create an account, the username you choose and your highest scores in games are stored on our server and shown to other players on leaderboards. If you write to us (feedback or email), what you write reaches us. Creating an account is optional.

1. Who this policy is for

This policy applies to the Muduco music-learning app for Android and iOS and its support service (destek@muduco.com). By installing and using the app you agree to what is described here.

Announcements and app settings

When the app opens, and from time to time, it fetches current announcements and app settings (for example a "new version available" notice or a maintenance message) from our own server at api.muduco.com. The request only contains your chosen language, whether your device runs Android or iOS, and the app version; nothing that identifies you is sent. Which announcements you have read is stored only on your device; we only see, as anonymous counts, how many times an announcement was viewed in total and how many times its button was tapped.

Anonymous usage counts

To improve the app, we learn aggregate totals only: on how many devices it is used, which lessons are finished or abandoned, which questions are hard and which games are played. These counts contain no identity, device ID, name, email or account; we cannot tell, and do not want to know, what any individual did. Counts may be split by language, platform and app version; very small groups are not shown in our dashboard. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Anonymous usage statistics.

Feedback and question reports

When you send the in-app form, we receive: your message, the type you chose (bug, suggestion, etc.), your email address if you enter it (so we can reply; it is optional), the app version, operating system version, platform, language and the screen you were on. For "Report question" we also receive the lesson, the question number and the reason you chose. Please do not include personal information in your message (for example your full name or phone number).

Notifications (optional)

If you turn on announcement notifications, your device's notification token (a random address), language, platform and app version are stored on our server so we can send notifications to your phone. Notifications are delivered through the Expo notification service and, depending on your phone, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification Service. The token is deleted from our server when you turn notifications off, reset the app or delete your account.

Microphone

The tuner and some games measure the pitch (frequency) of what you play or sing. The sound is analysed in your device's memory in real time and only a number — for example "440 Hz" — is used. Audio is not recorded, not written to a file and not sent to any server. When you close the app, no audio data remains.

Camera

The "Hand Chord" game lets you play chords with finger gestures. The image is processed on your device; only "which chord was played" is passed to the game. No photos or videos are taken, stored or sent to any server.

The open-source library that recognises hand gestures (Google MediaPipe) is bundled with the app on Android. On iOS it may be downloaded from jsDelivr and Google servers the first time you open the game; as with any web request, your device's IP address is visible to those servers during the download. No camera image or other personal data is sent.

Username and leaderboards. When you create an account you are asked to create a username. In games that have a leaderboard, only each player's highest score is listed; other players see only your username and your score. Your email address, profile name, lesson progress and other information are not shown to anyone. The name you enter in the app (your profile name) stays on your device and is not sent to our server. We recommend that you do not use your real name, surname or other identifying information in your username. You can change your username up to two times later; if something needs to be corrected, just write to destek@muduco.com. We block inappropriate names with a filter; you can report a name we missed with the report button on the leaderboard. We may remove an inappropriate name and remove an account that breaks the rules from leaderboards. Scores are kept separate from the anonymous usage counts and are never combined with them.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use ad networks.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools that identify you; we only keep the anonymous aggregate counts described above.
  • We don't store your IP address. (As with every internet request, it is processed momentarily by our hosting provider Cloudflare while the request reaches our server; we do not keep it or link it to any data.)
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data for marketing.
  • We don't access your location.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos or files.
  • We don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

4. Services that process your data

These services process data on our behalf and only for the purposes described here:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — sign-in and account data if you create an account. Details
  • Sign in with Google — if you create an account with Google, Google shares your name, email address and the link to your profile photo; these are kept in your Firebase account. When you delete your account we also revoke the app's access to your Google account. Details
  • Sign in with Apple (iOS) — Apple shares only your name and email address; you can hide your email and use Apple's relay address. When you delete your account we also revoke the Apple authorisation. Details
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) — our server api.muduco.com, anonymous counts, feedback, notification tokens, usernames, leaderboard scores and support emails run and are stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Details
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — crash and error reports; no personal data. Details
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification Service — only if you turn on notifications, to deliver them to your phone.
  • Google (Gmail) and Resend (Resend, Inc.) — when we receive support emails and send you replies.

We transfer no data to any other third party. Your username and your leaderboard score, however, are visible to everyone who uses the app. Some of these services run on servers outside Turkey (for example in the USA and the EU).

5. Guest use

You can choose "continue as guest" without creating an account. Your lesson progress then stays only on your device and no personal data that identifies you reaches us. Only if you send feedback yourself or turn on notifications does the information described above reach us. As a guest you can view leaderboards but cannot join them; your scores are not sent to us. If you delete the app, the data on your device is deleted too.

6. Your right to deletion

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes both your Firebase account and all progress on your device. If you signed in with Google or Apple, the app's Google or Apple authorisation is revoked as well. Your notification token, your username and your leaderboard scores are also deleted. Your username is not given to anyone else for 30 days; after that it can be taken again.
  • To delete only the data on your device: Settings → Reset all data. Your account, your username and your leaderboard scores are kept.
  • By email: If you cannot access the app, or want feedback or an email you sent us deleted, write to destek@muduco.com. We will complete your request within 30 days.

Anonymous usage counts cannot be matched to anyone, so they cannot be deleted per person; they contain no information about you.

7. How long data is kept

  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Feedback and support emails: at most 24 months; earlier if you ask us to delete them.
  • Notification token: until you turn notifications off or do not open the app for 9 months.
  • Your username and leaderboard scores: until you delete your account. Weekly leaderboards are deleted after 8 weeks. A name removed for being inappropriate is kept so that it cannot be taken again.
  • Anonymous usage counts: indefinitely, as aggregate totals.
  • Progress on your device: until you delete or reset the app.
  • Microphone and camera data: never stored.

Data deleted from our server may remain in the infrastructure's short-term recovery copies for up to 30 more days; these copies are not used for any other purpose.

8. Your rights under KVKK and GDPR

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to object to its processing and to data portability. To use these rights, contact us at destek@muduco.com.

Legal basis: we process account data for the performance of the contract needed to provide the service; feedback and emails at your request, so we can reply; anonymous usage counts on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the app, with your right to turn them off; and the notification token with your consent (when you turn notifications on).

9. Children's privacy

Muduco is designed for learners of all ages, but we do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13. We recommend that children use the app in guest mode and do not write an email address or personal information in the feedback form. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child sent us data, write to destek@muduco.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. If there is an important change, we will tell you in the app. The "last updated" date at the top always shows the current version.

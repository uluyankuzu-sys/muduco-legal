Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Muduco does not track you. We use no ad networks, no analytics, and no tracking tools. Data from your microphone and camera never leaves your device. You can use the app without an account, as a guest — in that case no personal data reaches us at all. One exception: if the app crashes, a technical error report is sent; it contains no personal data.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Muduco music education app published for Android and iOS. By installing and using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2. What data we process

Microphone

The tuner and some games measure the pitch (frequency) of the sound you play or sing. The audio is analysed momentarily in your device's memory, and all that comes out is a single number — for example "440 Hz". The audio is not recorded, not written to any file, and never sent to any server. When you close the app, no audio data remains.

Camera

The "Hand Chords" game lets you play chords with finger gestures. The video is processed on your device; only "which chord was played" reaches the game. No photo or video is captured, stored, or sent to any server.

The open-source hand-tracking library (Google MediaPipe) and its hand model are downloaded from jsDelivr and Google servers when you open the game. As with any web request, your device's IP address is visible to those servers during the download; no camera image or other personal data is sent.

3. What we do not do

  • We show no ads and use no ad networks.
  • We use no analytics or usage tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data for marketing.
  • We do not access your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or files.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

4. Who we share data with

If you choose to create an account, authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; your email address and password hash are stored on Google's servers. Google processes this data on our behalf. Details: Firebase privacy information.

If you choose Sign in with Apple (iOS), Apple shares only your name and email address, and you can hide your email behind Apple's relay address. When you delete your account we also revoke the Sign in with Apple authorisation. Details: Apple privacy policy.

If the app fails unexpectedly, a technical error report is sent to us through Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.). The report contains only the error itself and technical information such as device model and OS version; no personal data is sent. Details: Sentry privacy policy.

We transfer data to no other third party. The app communicates with no server other than Firebase, Sentry, the Google or Apple sign-in service you choose, and the "Hand Chords" library download (jsDelivr, Google).

5. Guest use

You can tap "continue as guest" without creating an account. In that case no personal data reaches us or Google; all your progress stays on your device only. If you uninstall the app, that data is gone too.

6. Your right to deletion

There are several routes:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This permanently deletes both your Firebase account and all progress on your device. If you signed in with Apple, the Apple authorisation is revoked as well.
  • To erase device data only: Settings → Reset All Data. Your account is kept.
  • By email: If you cannot access the app, write to destek@muduco.com from your account's email address. We will action your request within 30 days.

After deletion, your account and the data attached to it cannot be recovered.

7. How long data is kept

Your account is kept until you delete it. Progress on your device stays there until you uninstall or reset the app. Microphone and camera data are never retained at all.

8. Your rights under GDPR and similar laws

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to the processing of, and port your data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address above.

Legal basis: where you create an account, we process your email address on the basis of performance of a contract — it is necessary to provide the service to you.

9. Children's privacy

Muduco is designed for learners of all ages, but we do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13. If you are a parent or guardian who believes your child has sent us data, write to the address above and we will delete it. We recommend that children use the app in guest mode, where no personal data is collected at all.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. If a change is significant we will notify you in the app. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
